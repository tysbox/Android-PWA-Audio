Impulse Response files in this folder:
- hall.wav
- jazz_club.wav
- large_bottle_hall.wav
- st_nicolaes_church.wav

Source: Voxengo Free Reverb Impulse Responses
Original: http://www.voxengo.com/impulses/
Mirror used: https://github.com/oramics/sampled (IR/Voxengo/samples/)

Processing applied to the originals:
1. Resampled from 44.1kHz to 48kHz to match this app's fixed CamillaDSP
   pipeline samplerate.
2. Energy-normalized (scaled so sum-of-squares per channel ~= 1, i.e. ~0dB
   convolution gain) plus an additional 6dB safety headroom. The originals
   were peak-normalized to ~0dB but had convolution ENERGY of +14dB to
   +24dB -- peak level alone does not indicate convolution gain, which
   depends on the impulse's total energy. Using the un-normalized files
   caused massive, unintended level boosts when convolved with program
   material.
